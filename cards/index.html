travel cards

Two cards I always recommend for travelers: Revolut and Wise. Here’s how I use them and why they’re useful.

💡 Quick tip: I usually keep both cards.
  • 💳 Revolut: great for payments abroad and free currency exchange (up to a limit).
  • 💳 Wise: cheaper for international transfers and handy when receiving money.
With both, you avoid hidden fees and always have a backup card in case one doesn’t work.
💳 Get Revolut No-fee card for everyday spending abroad. 💳 Get Wise Low-fee card + cheap international transfers.
